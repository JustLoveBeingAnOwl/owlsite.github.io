Owl Fact of the day: 10/3/2026

The Blakiston's Fish Owl is the largest owl Species in the world. Males can stand up to 28 inches (~71cm) tall with a wingspan of 2 meters 4 kg of mass (8.81 lb).

Females can be even larger.
